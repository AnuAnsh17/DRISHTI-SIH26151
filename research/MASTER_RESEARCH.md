# Master Research Document
## SIH26151 — Dark web threat actor de-anonymization

### Project Overview
This document serves as the central research repository for the SIH26151 project: Dark web threat actor de-anonymization platform for National Technical Research Organisation (NTRO) under Theme: Blockchain & Cybersecurity.

### Research Mission
Perform complete technical reconnaissance of the ecosystem around dark web intelligence, threat actor attribution, and related technologies before beginning implementation.

### Key Principles
- Evidence-driven, not AI-opaque attribution
- Modular entity-resolution engine
- Temporal modeling of actor identities
- Principled confidence scoring
- Synthetic dark-web lab for evaluation
- Separation of hard identifiers from soft signals
- Explainable attribution with provenance tracking
